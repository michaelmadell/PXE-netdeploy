wimlib-imagex.exe and libwim-15.dll in this folder are unmodified binaries
of wimlib, from the Windows release at https://wimlib.net/ .

netdeploy runs wimlib-imagex as a separate program to build boot images.

Source code: the same version's source is at https://wimlib.net/downloads/
(wimlib-<version>.tar.gz); the version is in README-wimlib.txt and is
printed by "wimlib-imagex --version".
